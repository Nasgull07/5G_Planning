Final BBU pool node selection (K=10)

The selected nodes are Site IDs: 040008, 012905, 0805740, 000604, CATR1045B, 0802121, CATR5851AJOS, 0800532, 0803252, 0802479. They were chosen from the 24 distinct real sites selected by the five independent K=10 runs (s=0, 25, 50, 75, and 100). A greedy selection and swap-refinement search chose one fixed set of ten sites to minimize the equally weighted average of mean straight-line RRH-to-nearest-pool distances across all five scenarios. Site 0802479 appeared in all five scenario selections; 040008 and 012905 appeared in four. With the final fixed set, scenario mean distances range from 2.49 to 2.82 km, and the maximum assigned distance is 8.98 km in every scenario. This distance-based choice uses only the available locations; it does not account for traffic, capacity, fibre routes, or site feasibility. Synthetic RRHs were included as stress-test demand points, but the selected pool nodes are real sites. The CSV lists each node and its assigned RRH count in each scenario.
